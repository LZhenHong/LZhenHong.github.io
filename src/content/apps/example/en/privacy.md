# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-20

## Overview

This privacy policy applies to Example App ("the app"). It explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

## Information the App Collects

The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All data you create stays on your device.

## Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

- Apple App Store / StoreKit — handles purchases. See [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Data Retention and Deletion

No personal data leaves the device, so there is nothing to delete on our side. Deleting the app removes all of its local data.

## Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
