# Refund Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026.

## No subscription, nothing to cancel

The VirtualLocation VIP license is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. There is no recurring billing and nothing to cancel.

## Requesting a refund

Within 14 days of your purchase, email [leeden.dev@gmail.com](mailto:leeden.dev@gmail.com) with your Dodo Payments order ID, and the refund will be issued back to your original payment method via Dodo Payments.

## What a refund does not change

The license is a buyout that works offline once activated. A refund does not remotely revoke a license already activated on your Mac; it affects new activations of the refunded license key.

Questions about refunds: [leeden.dev@gmail.com](mailto:leeden.dev@gmail.com).
