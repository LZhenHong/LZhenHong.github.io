## The app

VirtualLocation is a developer tool: it sets a virtual location on an iPhone or iPad connected to your Mac, over USB or the same Wi-Fi network, using Apple's own developer services. It requires no jailbreak and installs nothing on the device. It is intended for development, testing, and debugging.

## Free tier and VIP license

The Teleport feature is free. Move, Joystick, and batch operations require a VIP license. The VIP license is a one-time purchase — not a subscription — with no expiration date. You activate it by entering a license key in the app; once activated, it works offline.

Each license supports up to 3 activated Macs at a time. Connected iPhone and iPad devices can still be managed in batches. To move an activation to another Mac, remove the license from an old Mac while online. If the old Mac is unavailable or its activation cannot be released, contact support.

Please don't share your license key publicly or redistribute the app as your own work.
