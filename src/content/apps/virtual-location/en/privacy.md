# Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026.

The short version: by default, nothing leaves your Mac. VirtualLocation has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.

## What data is collected, and how

- **Location simulation stays local.** Setting a virtual location talks to your device over a local (127.0.0.1) channel only.
- **Your content is stored on your Mac.** Favorite places, saved routes, and search history live in the app's local storage.
- **iCloud sync (on by default).** Your favorite places, saved routes, and search history sync across your Macs through your private iCloud (CloudKit) database. You can turn it off in the app's Settings. Your license, crash-reporting choice, device favorites, and map preferences never sync.
- **Crash reports (off by default).** If you turn on "Share Crash Reports" in Settings, the app sends minimal crash stack traces or stable error codes, plus the app version, over HTTPS. No local logs are attached.
- **Update checks.** The app downloads a public appcast file over HTTPS to compare versions, and installs an update only after you confirm.
- **VIP license.** Activating a license contacts the payment provider once with your license key, and an activation ID is recorded. After that the license works offline.

## How the data is used

- iCloud data: to make your places, routes, and history available on your other Macs.
- Crash reports: to find and fix bugs.
- License activation: to verify your purchase.

Nothing is used for advertising, tracking, or sale, and nothing is used to profile you.

## Third parties

- **Apple iCloud / CloudKit** holds your synced data in your private database, under your iCloud account.
- **Sentry** receives crash reports only if you opt in; the project is configured not to store IP addresses and to strip location fields.
- **Dodo Payments** processes purchases and license activation.
- **GitHub** hosts the update feed and the download files.

Each of these receives only the data described above, and protects it under its own privacy policy.

## Retention and deletion

- Data on your Mac stays until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
- Synced data lives in your iCloud account. Deleting an item in the app deletes it from iCloud; turning off sync stops all future syncing.
- To remove a VIP license, use Remove License in the app — it deactivates the license locally and releases the activation remotely where possible.
- You can email me at any time to ask for deletion of any data tied to you, such as a crash report.

## Withdrawing consent

- Crash reports: turn off "Share Crash Reports" in Settings; nothing more is sent.
- iCloud sync: turn it off in Settings. Data already synced stays in your iCloud account until you delete it.
- License: use Remove License in the app.
