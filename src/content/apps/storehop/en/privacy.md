# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-24

## Overview

This privacy policy applies to StoreHop ("the app"), a utility for browsing regional App Store storefronts, developed by Eden. It explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

## Information the App Collects

**StoreHop does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.** There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

## Data Stored on Your Device

Your preferences — enabled storefronts, favorites, your primary storefront, and recent switch history — are stored locally in a shared container on your device so the app, its widgets, and its controls see the same state. This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app removes the app itself; on Mac, the settings stay behind and can be removed by running `defaults delete group.io.lzhlovesjyq.StoreHop` in Terminal.

## Network Access

The app itself makes no network connections. When you switch storefronts, the app asks the operating system to open an App Store link; any network activity from that point on is between the App Store app and Apple, and is subject to [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Third-Party Services

The app integrates no third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. Downloading the app through the App Store is subject to [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
