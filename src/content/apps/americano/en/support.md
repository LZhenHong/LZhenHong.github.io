# Americano Support

Americano is a macOS menu bar app that keeps your Mac awake — handy during downloads, presentations, or any long-running task.

## Contact

- Email: [leeden.dev@gmail.com](mailto:leeden.dev@gmail.com)
- GitHub Issues: [LZhenHong/Americano](https://github.com/LZhenHong/Americano/issues)

When reporting an issue, please include your macOS version, your Americano version (menu → Settings → About), and where you installed the app from (Mac App Store, GitHub, or Homebrew).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What's the difference between left-click and right-click?**
A: Right-click the cup icon toggles sleep prevention on or off. Left-click opens the menu, where you pick a duration or open Settings (⌘,).

**Q: The menu bar icon's status looks wrong.**
A: If you use Bartender, Ice, or a similar menu bar manager and have hidden the Americano icon, its status display may be inaccurate. Unhide the icon to see the real state.

**Q: Mac App Store version vs. the GitHub/Homebrew version?**
A: Same app, same bundle id — only one copy can be installed at a time, and settings carry over. The GitHub/Homebrew build updates itself via Sparkle; the Mac App Store build is updated through the App Store and runs inside the App Sandbox.

**Q: How do I see the onboarding again?**
A: Open Settings → About and choose to re-open the onboarding.
