# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-27

## Overview

This privacy policy applies to SettingsHop ("the app"). It explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

## Information the App Collects

The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. It makes no network requests, has no accounts, and includes no analytics or advertising SDKs.

## Information Stored on Your Device

Your favorite panes, recently opened panes, and widget configuration are stored locally in `UserDefaults`, shared between the app and its widget extension through an App Group container. This data never leaves your Mac.

## Third-Party Services

The app uses no third-party services. When you open a pane, the app hands a `x-apple.systempreferences:` URL to macOS, which opens System Settings — that interaction happens entirely on your device and is governed by macOS, not by the app. See [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Data Retention and Deletion

All app data lives on your device. Removing the app deletes its settings; you can also clear favorites inside the app at any time. For deletion questions, email the address below.

## Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
