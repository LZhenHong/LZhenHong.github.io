## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I open the search panel?**

A: Click the gear icon in the menu bar, or press Option-Space anywhere.

**Q: How do favorites and recents work?**

A: Star any result to pin it. Favorites and recently opened panes always sort first, and they drive the "Favorite Settings" widget.

**Q: A pane is missing from the list (e.g. Touch ID).**

A: Panes that need hardware your Mac doesn't have (Touch ID, Battery, Trackpad) are hidden automatically — they wouldn't open anyway.

**Q: Does SettingsHop work offline?**

A: Yes. The app never touches the network; every jump is a local deep link into System Settings.
